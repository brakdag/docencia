# Role Management Policies

## Overview
This document defines the standards and guidelines for defining and managing roles within the project. To ensure scalability and clarity, all roles must follow a standardized documentation format.

## Role Documentation Standards
Every role defined in the `docs/staff/` directory must include the following sections:

1. **Role Title**: A clear, concise name for the position.
2. **Purpose**: A high-level description of why the role exists and its primary objective.
3. **Responsibilities**: A bulleted list of specific tasks and expectations.
4. **Required Competencies**: The skills, knowledge, or experience necessary to perform the role.
5. **Workflow**: How the person in this role interacts with the project and other team members.

## General Guidelines
- **Language**: All staff documentation must be written in English to ensure professional standardization.
- **Accessibility**: Role definitions should be written such that a new member can understand their expectations without external guidance.
- **Maintenance**: Roles should be reviewed and updated whenever the project's scope or the organizational structure changes.

## Governance and Authority
- **Ultimate Authority**: The Project Lead is the maximum authority for this project.
- **Prior Approval**: All changes, modifications, or additions to the project material, structure, or roles must be consulted and approved by the Project Lead before implementation.
- **No Automatic Actions**: No team member or agent is permitted to perform actions or apply changes automatically without explicit, prior consent.
Changes to role definitions must be reviewed by the Project Lead to ensure alignment with the overall educational goals of the Professional Practices guide.
