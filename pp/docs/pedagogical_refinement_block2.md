## 1. Transformation of "Idea de Proyecto"

### A. From "List" to "Methodology"
The current text tells students *what* to include in an idea, but not *how* to arrive at it. To bridge this gap, I propose introducing a simplified **Technical Diagnosis Tool**.

**Pedagogical Change**: 
- Introduce the concept of the **"Problem Tree"** (Root Cause $ightarrow$ Problem $ightarrow$ Effect). This gives students a visual and logical method to justify their project idea.
- Replace the generic "Conceptualization" list with a **"Project Idea Canvas"**. This is a structured one-page template where students must define: *The Problem, The Target User, Technical Constraints, and the Expected Impact*.

### B. Activity Redesign: The "Idea Pitch"
Instead of a research questionnaire, I propose a **"Project Ideation Workshop"**.

**The New Activity Structure:**
1. **Observation Phase**: Students identify a real problem in the school workshop or a local industry.
2. **Diagnosis Phase**: Use the Problem Tree to find the root cause.
3. **Proposal Phase**: Fill out the Project Idea Canvas.
4. **Validation**: A brief "Pitch" to the professor to verify technical viability before proceeding.

**Benefit**: This transforms a passive research task into a professional engineering process.

## 2. Transformation of "Tarjetas Kanban"

### A. Contextual Alignment (The "Workshop Soul")
The software example is a cognitive barrier. We must translate Kanban into the language of the workshop.

**Pedagogical Change**: 
- **Industrialized Flow**: Replace "Backlog/Doing/Done" with a **Workshop Production Line**:
  `Ideation` $ightarrow$ `Design/Plans` $ightarrow$ `Material Procurement` $ightarrow$ `Fabrication/Assembly` $ightarrow$ `Testing/QA` $ightarrow$ `Finished`.
- **Concrete Analogy for WIP Limits**: Explain WIP (Work In Progress) limits using the analogy of **"Workshop Space"**. 
  *Example*: "If we have 5 machines in the 'Assembly' stage but only 2 workbenches, we have a bottleneck. WIP limits prevent the workshop from becoming a warehouse of half-finished projects."

### B. Activity Redesign: "Design Your Board"
Replace the comprehension questionnaire with a **"Kanban Implementation Exercise"**.

**The New Activity Structure:**
1. **Task Breakdown**: Students list all the physical tasks needed for their specific project (e.g., "Cut steel profiles", "Solder circuit", "Paint frame").
2. **Board Design**: Students draw their own Kanban board, defining the columns that make sense for *their* specific project flow.
3. **WIP Definition**: Students must decide and justify a WIP limit for their most critical stage (e.g., "Only 2 parts can be in the Welding stage at once").

**Benefit**: The student stops seeing Kanban as a "software thing" and starts seeing it as a tool to manage their own physical project.
