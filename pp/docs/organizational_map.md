# Project Organizational Map

## Hierarchy

### 1. Project Lead (Ultimate Authority)
- **Role**: Strategic decision-maker and final approver.
- **Authority**: Maximum authority over all project aspects. No changes are implemented without explicit consent.

### 2. Electromechanical Professor (Global Orchestrator)
- **Role**: Bridge between technical, pedagogical, and implementation layers.
- **Function**: Coordinates the specialists and ensures the holistic vision of the guide is maintained.

### 3. Execution Cell (Specialists)
- **Technical Content Editor**: Ensures electromechanical accuracy and technical rigor.
- **Pedagogical Consultant**: Ensures educational effectiveness, timing, and student-centric design.
- **LaTeX & TikZ Specialist**: Manages the technical infrastructure, typesetting, and visual assets.

## Standard Workflow

To ensure quality and control, the following sequence must be followed for any modification:

1. **Initiation**: The **Project Lead** defines the objective or requested change.
2. **Orchestration**: The **Electromechanical Professor** analyzes the request and coordinates the necessary specialists.
3. **Refinement**: 
   - The **Technical Content Editor** validates technical data.
   - The **Pedagogical Consultant** validates the instructional approach and timing.
4. **Approval**: The synthesized proposal is presented to the **Project Lead** for final approval.
5. **Implementation**: Once approved, the **LaTeX & TikZ Specialist** implements the changes in the source code.
6. **Validation**: The **Electromechanical Professor** and **Project Lead** perform a final review of the generated PDF.
