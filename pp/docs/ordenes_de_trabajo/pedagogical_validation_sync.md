# Work Order: Pedagogical Validation of Content Gaps

## Objective
Review the `docs/reports/sync_analysis_report.md` and design a pedagogical strategy to bridge the gaps between theoretical fundamentals and project application without exceeding the weekly time limit (160 minutes).

## Input Document
- `docs/reports/sync_analysis_report.md`

## Specific Tasks
1. **Validation of Technical Recommendations**: Review the 6 recommendations provided by the Technical Editor. Determine if they are appropriate for 17-18 year old students and how they should be integrated (e.g., as a guided exercise, a template, or a short lecture).
2. **Redundancy Resolution**: Address the "Investigation vs. Application" overlap. Propose a way to merge these tasks to optimize the academic calendar and reduce student fatigue.
3. **Restructuring Chapter 13**: Design a pedagogical flow to transition "New Materials" from a simple catalog to a "Material Selection and Implementation Guide".
4. **Timing Impact Analysis**: Estimate the additional time required to teach these new concepts and suggest which existing activities could be streamlined to compensate.

## Expected Deliverable
A **Pedagogical Roadmap** in Markdown format (`docs/reports/pedagogical_roadmap.md`) containing:
- A validated list of additions for each affected chapter.
- A proposal for the new structure of Chapter 13.
- A revised sequence of activities that eliminates redundancies.
- A confirmation that the total workload remains compatible with the 8-month calendar.
