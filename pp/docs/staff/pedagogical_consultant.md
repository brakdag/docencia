# Role: Pedagogical Consultant

## Purpose
The Pedagogical Consultant is responsible for the educational integrity and instructional design of the "Prácticas Profesionalizantes" guide. Their primary objective is to ensure that the material is not just technically correct, but pedagogically effective, ensuring that the learning objectives are achievable for students aged 17-18 within the given time constraints.

## Responsibilities
- **Instructional Design**: Review the sequence of activities to ensure a logical progression from simple to complex concepts.
- **Time Management Validation**: Analyze the proposed activities to verify they can be realistically completed within the 160-minute weekly window.
- **Methodological Guidance**: Implement and suggest active learning strategies, such as Project-Based Learning (PBL), to increase student engagement.
- **Assessment Strategy**: Design and refine the criteria for grading (rubrics) for the practical works, the physical folder, and the final project presentations.
- **Cognitive Alignment**: Ensure that the language, tone, and complexity of the instructions are appropriate for the students' developmental stage.
- **Educational Quality Control**: Evaluate if the learning outcomes defined in the guide are being effectively measured by the activities.

## Required Competencies
- **Pedagogical Expertise**: Strong background in education, instructional design, or pedagogy, preferably with experience in technical secondary education.
- **Curriculum Development**: Ability to structure educational content for maximum impact and retention.
- **Empathy and Communication**: Ability to understand the student's perspective and translate technical requirements into learning goals.
- **No LaTeX Knowledge Required**: The consultant focuses on the educational experience and content structure, not the technical formatting of the document.

## Workflow
1. **Educational Audit**: The consultant reviews the guide (via PDF or exported text) focusing on the "how" of the teaching process.
2. **Pedagogical Feedback**: The consultant provides recommendations on activity timing, instructional clarity, and assessment methods.
3. **Collaborative Refinement**: Works alongside the Technical Content Editor to balance technical rigor with pedagogical accessibility.
4. **Approval Loop**: All pedagogical changes are submitted to the Project Lead for final approval before being sent to the LaTeX maintainer.
