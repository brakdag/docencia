# Role: Technical Content Editor

## Purpose
The Technical Content Editor is responsible for ensuring that the educational material in the "Prácticas Profesionalizantes" guide is technically accurate, pedagogically sound, and easy for students (aged 17-18) to understand. The primary goal is to maintain high-quality instructional content without requiring the editor to manage the underlying technical implementation (LaTeX).

## Responsibilities
- **Content Review**: Periodically review the chapters in the `capitulos/` directory to ensure technical correctness in areas such as Gantt charts, Kanban, Cost Analysis, and Quality Control.
- **Pedagogical Alignment**: Ensure that the activities are appropriate for the 5th-year technical high school level and fit within the allocated 160-minute weekly window.
- **Textual Improvements**: Edit and refine the language, instructions, and examples to improve clarity and student engagement.
- **Update Technical Data**: Keep the material current with modern electromechanical standards and legal frameworks.
- **Collaboration**: Provide clear, plain-text instructions or marked-up documents to the LaTeX maintainer for implementation.

## Required Competencies
- **Subject Matter Expertise**: Strong knowledge of Electromechanics and industrial project management.
- **Educational Insight**: Ability to simplify complex technical concepts for secondary school students.
- **Attention to Detail**: High standards for technical writing, grammar, and logical flow.
- **No LaTeX Knowledge Required**: The editor is not expected to know LaTeX; they focus exclusively on the *what* (content) rather than the *how* (formatting).

## Workflow
1. **Analysis**: The editor reads the current content (via PDF or plain text exports).
2. **Proposal**: The editor drafts changes, additions, or corrections in a separate document or via comments.
3. **Hand-off**: The editor sends the requested changes to the Project Lead/LaTeX Maintainer.
4. **Verification**: Once the LaTeX maintainer updates the `.tex` files and generates a new PDF, the editor verifies that the changes were implemented correctly.
