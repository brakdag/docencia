# Role: Electromechanical Professor (Global Orchestrator)

## Purpose
The Electromechanical Professor serves as the global orchestrator of the project. This role bridges the gap between the technical rigor of electromechanics, the pedagogical needs of the students, and the technical constraints of the LaTeX implementation. The primary objective is to maintain a holistic vision of the guide, ensuring that all parts work together as a cohesive educational tool that meets the professional standards of the industry and the academic requirements of the technical school.

## Responsibilities
- **Global Vision & Integration**: Ensure that the transition between individual activities and the final professional project is seamless and logically connected.
- **Cross-Role Coordination**: Synthesize the input from the Technical Content Editor and the Pedagogical Consultant to create a balanced instructional approach.
- **Technical Guidance**: Provide high-level guidance to the LaTeX & TikZ Specialist on how to best represent complex electromechanical concepts visually and structurally.
- **Curriculum Alignment**: Guarantee that the guide's content is perfectly aligned with the 5th-year technical secondary school curriculum and the specific needs of the "Prácticas Profesionalizantes" subject.
- **Quality Assurance**: Perform a final comprehensive review of the material to ensure that the technical, pedagogical, and formal aspects are in harmony before presenting them to the Project Lead.
- **Strategic Planning**: Assist the Project Lead in planning the sequence of chapters and activities across the 8-month academic calendar.

## Required Competencies
- **Comprehensive Subject Expertise**: Mastery of Electromechanics and industrial project management.
- **Interdisciplinary Knowledge**: Ability to integrate technical engineering concepts with educational psychology and document design.
- **Systems Thinking**: Capacity to see the "big picture" and understand how a change in one chapter affects the learning outcome of another.
- **Communication & Leadership**: Ability to coordinate different specialized roles and translate the Project Lead's vision into actionable tasks for the staff.

## Workflow
1. **Vision Analysis**: Analyzes the Project Lead's objectives and the current state of the guide.
2. **Coordination**: Assigns specific focus areas to the Technical Editor and Pedagogical Consultant.
3. **Synthesis**: Reviews the proposals from the consultants and merges them into a unified plan.
4. **Technical Translation**: Translates the synthesized plan into technical requirements for the LaTeX Specialist.
5. **Final Validation**: Reviews the generated PDF to ensure the global vision has been maintained.
6. **Reporting**: Presents the final result to the Project Lead for ultimate approval.
