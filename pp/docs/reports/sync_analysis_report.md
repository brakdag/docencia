## 2. Gaps Identified

### Technical Concepts Missing in Individual Section:
- **Project Idea**: The use of a **Weighting Matrix (Matriz de Ponderación)** to select the best technical alternative is requested in the project but not explained in the theory.
- **Kanban**: The theory focuses on the board and WIP. The project asks for **assignment logic, notification systems, and information security**, which are not addressed.
- **Market Study**: The concept of **Market Elasticity** is used as a requirement in the project but is only listed as a question for the student to research, not as a taught concept.
- **Maintenance**: The **Equipment Sheet (Ficha de Equipo)** and the use of **QR codes** for asset management are requested as deliverables but no template or methodology is provided in the theory.
- **Location**: The **Weighted Factor Method (Sistema de Puntuación)** for choosing a location is requested but not taught.
- **New Materials**: There is a critical gap here. The individual chapter is a descriptive list of materials (Carbon fiber, Coltan, etc.), while the project asks for a full **R&D Project Plan** (milestones, economic viability, characterization methods, and sustainability). The students are not taught *how* to conduct this research.

## 3. Redundancies

- **Investigation vs. Application**: Many "Investigate and Answer" activities in the individual chapters are identical to the requirements of the project chapters. While this reinforces learning, it can feel repetitive. It is recommended to move the "Investigation" part of the individual chapter into a "Preliminary Research" phase of the project itself.

## 4. Technical Recommendations

1. **Enhance Chapter 02 (Idea)**: Include a brief tutorial on how to create a decision matrix (criteria $\rightarrow$ weights $\rightarrow$ scores) to justify the selection of the "Optimized Base Solution".
2. **Expand Chapter 02 (Kanban)**: Add a section on "Team Coordination", explaining how tasks are assigned and how progress is communicated in an industrial setting.
3. **Formalize Maintenance Tools**: Provide a standard template for the "Equipment Sheet" in Chapter 07 so students have a technical baseline to follow.
4. **Add Decision Tools to Location**: Include the "Weighted Factor Method" in Chapter 12 to provide a mathematical basis for the location choice.
5. **Restructure New Materials**: Change Chapter 13 from a "Material Catalog" to a "Guide for Material Selection and Implementation". Instead of just describing Coltan or Carbon Fiber, teach them how to compare materials based on properties (strength, weight, cost) and how to plan a material trial/test.
6. **Pedagogical Shift**: Convert some of the theoretical "Investigate" questions into "Guided Examples" to reduce the cognitive load on the student before they hit the project phase.
