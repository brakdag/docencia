# Pedagogical Roadmap: Bridging Technical Gaps

## 1. Introduction
This roadmap outlines the pedagogical strategy to align theoretical content with project requirements, ensuring that students (aged 17-18) have the necessary tools to complete their "Prácticas Profesionalizantes" without cognitive overload or time deficits.

## 2. Validated Technical Additions

Based on the `sync_analysis_report.md`, the following additions are validated and categorized by instructional method:

| Chapter | Addition | Pedagogical Method | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **02 (Idea)** | Decision/Weighting Matrix | **Guided Exercise** | Teach students how to objectively justify a technical choice using criteria and weights. |
| **02 (Kanban)** | Team Coordination & Security | **Short Lecture / Discussion** | Explain industrial communication flows and the importance of information security in task management. |
| **07 (Maint.)** | Equipment Sheet Template | **Template Application** | Provide a standardized technical document to ensure consistency in asset registration. |
| **12 (Loc.)** | Weighted Factor Method | **Guided Exercise** | Provide a mathematical basis for location selection, moving beyond intuitive choices. |
| **13 (Mat.)** | Material Selection Guide | **PBL (Project-Based Learning)** | Transition from passive reading (catalog) to active research (R&D plan). |

## 3. Restructuring Chapter 13: "New Materials"

To resolve the mismatch between the "Catalog" theory and the "R&D Plan" project, Chapter 13 will be restructured as follows:

**New Flow: Material Selection and Implementation Guide**
1. **Material Properties (Theoretical Foundation)**: Brief overview of key properties (mechanical, thermal, chemical) instead of a list of materials.
2. **Comparative Analysis (The Tool)**: Introduction of a comparison matrix to evaluate materials against project needs (e.g., Strength vs. Cost).
3. **R&D Planning (The Application)**: A step-by-step guide on how to design a material trial, including:
    - Defining milestones.
    - Establishing characterization methods.
    - Assessing economic viability and sustainability.

## 4. Redundancy Resolution & Activity Sequence

To optimize the 160-minute weekly window and reduce student fatigue, the following shift in sequence is implemented:

**Old Sequence**: `Theory` $\rightarrow$ `Investigation (Standalone)` $\rightarrow$ `Project Application`
**New Sequence**: `Theory` $\rightarrow$ `Guided Example` $\rightarrow$ `Project Application (Integrated Research)`

**Action Items**:
- **Merge Investigation**: All "Investigate and Answer" activities that overlap with project deliverables are removed as standalone tasks. They are now integrated as the "Preliminary Research" phase of the corresponding project chapter.
- **Cognitive Load Reduction**: Replace open-ended research questions with "Guided Examples" that show the expected technical standard before the student applies it to their project.

## 5. Timing Impact Analysis

| Change | Estimated Time Impact | Compensation Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| New Technical Tools (Matrices, Methods) | +40 min / chapter | Removed redundant investigation tasks (-60 min / chapter). |
| Chapter 13 Restructuring | +60 min (total) | Streamlined material catalog reading (-40 min). |
| **Net Impact** | **-20 to -40 min / week** | **Positive: Increases efficiency and reduces fatigue.** |

## 6. Final Confirmation
The proposed changes are fully compatible with the 8-month academic calendar. By converting passive research into active application, we maintain the technical rigor while ensuring the workload remains realistic for 17-18 year old students.
