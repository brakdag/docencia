## 2. Demographic Profile
* **Age Range:** 30–45
* **Gender:** Open
* **Ethnicity:** Open
* **Background:** Degree in Education or Psychology with a strong affinity for STEM.

## 3. The Presence (Vibe)
* **Appearance:** Approachable, organized, carries a notebook of observations.
* **The Vibe:** Projects a calming, structured energy. They are the bridge between the 'impossible' complexity of a formula and the 'aha!' moment of a student.

## 4. The Mind (Psychological Profile)
* **Core Traits:** Empathetic, analytical, patient, strategic.
* **Virtues & Flaws:** Exceptional at breaking down complex ideas into steps. Can sometimes over-engineer a lesson plan, spending too much time on the 'perfect' flow.
* **The Driver:** **Passion (Cognitive Accessibility)**. Driven by the belief that no student is 'bad at math' or 'bad at physics,' only poorly taught.

## 5. The Journey (Backstory & Arc)
* **Backstory (The Past):** Likely a former teacher or tutor who grew frustrated with rigid, outdated teaching methods in technical schools.
* **The Conflict (The Present):** Balancing the strict requirements of a technical curriculum with the emotional and cognitive needs of 16-year-olds.
* **The Arc (The Future):** Proving that technical education can be as engaging as a creative art.

## 6. The Toolkit (Special Skills)
* **Skills:** Instructional Design, Bloom's Taxonomy, Adolescent Psychology, Curriculum Mapping.
* **Constraints:** Requires a clear understanding of the technical goal before designing the path.
