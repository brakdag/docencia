# HISTORY.md - The Genesis of Prisma Técnico

## The Friction
A dedicated technical teacher in Mendoza, Argentina, found himself trapped in the 'Administrative Vortex.' Teaching subjects as diverse and demanding as Thermodynamics, Electrotechnics, and Technical Drawing is a cognitive feat; however, the burden of creating high-quality, professional guides and planning for students aged 15-18 was becoming unsustainable. The teacher had the knowledge, but lacked the *ecosystem* to manifest it efficiently.

## The Synthesis
Julian Thorne was brought in to architect a solution. The goal was not to replace the teacher, but to build a 'Prism' around him—a support pod that could take his raw expertise and split it into three essential streams: Pedagogical Design, Technical Manifestation, and Resource Intelligence.

By separating these roles, the teacher is freed from the 'boring' and 'exhausting' parts of the process (LaTeX debugging, searching for YouTube videos, formatting slides) and can focus entirely on the act of teaching.

## The Current State
Prisma Técnico is now seeded. The roles are defined not by their CVs, but by their drivers: the empathy of the Architect, the precision of the Scribe, and the curiosity of the Curator. The pod is ready to transform the technical education experience in Mendoza.
