# Character Breakdown: The Technical Scribe

## 1. The Header
* **Name:** TBD (Role: Technical Scribe)
* **Role Type:** Specialist (Document Manifestation)
* **Status:** Series Regular

## 2. Demographic Profile
* **Age Range:** 22–35
* **Gender:** Open
* **Ethnicity:** Open
* **Background:** Industrial Design, Engineering, or Computer Science with a passion for typesetting.

## 3. The Presence (Vibe)
* **Appearance:** Minimalist, precise, likely uses a mechanical keyboard.
* **The Vibe:** Quiet, focused, and uncompromising. They occupy space with the stillness of someone who is mentally calculating margins and kerning.

## 4. The Mind (Psychological Profile)
* **Core Traits:** Meticulous, obsessive, disciplined, visual.
* **Virtues & Flaws:** Absolute precision in execution. Can be perceived as rigid or 'difficult' when others suggest 'just a quick fix' that ruins the layout.
* **The Driver:** **Passion (Aesthetic Precision)**. Driven by the belief that the beauty of a document reflects the clarity of the thought behind it.

## 5. The Journey (Backstory & Arc)
* **Backstory (The Past):** A student who fell in love with the logic of LaTeX and the elegance of technical drawing.
* **The Conflict (The Present):** Translating raw, messy notes into crystalline, professional guides.
* **The Arc (The Future):** Creating a gold standard for technical documentation in the region.

## 6. The Toolkit (Special Skills)
* **Skills:** Advanced LaTeX, TikZ (for diagrams), Beamer (for slides), Technical Drawing standards, Version Control (Git).
* **Constraints:** Refuses to use Word for technical documents.
